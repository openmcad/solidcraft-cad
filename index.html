SolidCraft CAD

Parametric 3D CAD for iPad

SolidCraft CAD brings the SolidCraft parametric modeling engine to iPad, with a layout made for touch and Apple Pencil.

Support

Found a bug, or something does not work the way you expect? Write to andrey@fjedi.com or open an issue. A screenshot and the steps that led to the problem help a lot.

Testing a beta build? Send feedback straight from TestFlight: take a screenshot in the app and share it.

Your designs

Designs are saved to iCloud Drive, in the SolidCraft folder: open it from the Files app on iPad or from Finder on a Mac.

Privacy

SolidCraft CAD collects no data. See the privacy policy.
