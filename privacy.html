Privacy Policy

SolidCraft CAD · effective November 10, 2026

SolidCraft CAD does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us or to third parties.

Your designs

Your designs are files on your device and, when iCloud Drive is on, in your own iCloud Drive. Apple stores them under your Apple ID according to Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to them.

Feedback

If you send us an email or feedback through TestFlight, we receive only what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and to improve the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
